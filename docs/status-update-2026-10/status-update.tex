\noindent
This note collects the quantitative checks we have so far on the correctness of the
Green's functions $G_k$, the transfer functions $T_k$ and the source integral, together with
performance measurements of the adaptive Levin integrator. Errors labelled ``env'' are relative
to the local oscillation envelope. Each figure is the latest measurement on record; superseded
figures are quoted only where they show what has changed. The file paths in grey give the
repository document where each number is recorded.

\section{Green's functions and transfer functions}

\paragraph{Numerical $G_k$ against the exact radiation Green's function.}
Over all 50 production wavenumbers ($k = 10^5$--$3\times10^8\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}$), the worst
error at the production tolerances $(\mathrm{atol},\mathrm{rtol}) = (10^{-10},10^{-8})$ is
$2.6\times10^{-7}\envl$. The error tracks rtol cleanly (Table~\ref{tab:gk}). Self-convergence
and the oracle agree to a ratio of $1.00$ at every setting. We therefore trust self-convergence
on $\Lambda$CDM and on the QCD background, where no oracle exists; there the solver error is the
same, about $2.5\times10^{-7}$.

\paragraph{Numerical $T_k$ against $T = 3(\sin x - x\cos x)/x^3$.}
At the adopted rtol of $3\times10^{-11}$, the worst self-convergence error over 150
(model, $k$) runs is $3.9\times10^{-8}$. Against the exact $T$ the error is about
$2.6\times10^{-6}$. This floor comes from the super-horizon initial condition
($T=1$, $T'=0$ at $x_i \approx 0.004$), not from the solver: with exact initial data the
converged run matches the closed form to about $10^{-11}$.
\src{docs/tolerance-convergence/TK-NUMERIC-AND-EXIT-TIME.md}

\paragraph{Liouville--Green (WKB) representations.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{$G_k$ phase in radiation}, where the Liouville--Green form is exact: the error is
  about 2\,ulp, $3.7\times10^{-9}$\,rad over a span of $9\times10^6$\,rad. The previous
  two-stage phase ODE was $0.98$\,rad off over a span of $10^9$\,rad.
  \src{prompts/GkTk-remedial/logs/06-gk-wkb-phase-from-primitive.md}
\item \emph{$T_k$ truncation floor}: the error scales as $x_i^{-3}$ in the hand-over point,
  $3.8\times10^{-5}$ at $x_i=24$ and $7.8\times10^{-9}$ at $x_i=400$. At the production
  hand-over $x_T\approx15.5$ this implies about $1.4\times10^{-4}\envl$.
  \src{prompts/GkTk-remedial/logs/07-tk-wkb-phase-from-primitive.md}
\item \emph{Bessel-phase oracle}: about $10^{-12}$ for low orders, and accurate to
  $2\times10^{-16}$ up to $x\sim10^{15}$. The naive evaluation was $O(1)$ wrong beyond
  $x\sim10^{12}$.
  \src{docs/transfer-remedial-verification.md}
\item \emph{Stored WKB phases on $\Lambda$CDM and QCD}, compared against high-precision
  references: 1--2\,ulp of the phase span. Before remediation $\theta_G$ was off by up to
  $7\times10^3$\,rad at $k=3\times10^8$.
  \src{docs/gktk-remedial-verification.md, docs/qcd-background-verification.md}
\end{itemize}

\section{Source integral against analytic oracles}

We test the normalisation $N = -\tfrac98\,I_{\text{code}}/I_{\text{oracle}}$. In radiation
($b=0$) it should equal $-9/8$; for general $b$ it should equal
$N(b) = -(3+2b)^2/[2(2+b)^2]$. The fixture spans $x = k\tau$ from 4 to $1.6\times10^3$ and
$u = q/k$ from $0.01$ to $12$, including squeezed and non-closable configurations.

Notes on the oracles:
\begin{itemize}
\item In double precision, Kohri--Terada eq.~(22) is itself accurate only to about $10^{-10}$
  at $u=0.01$, which is why it is evaluated at 50 digits. Its small-$x$ limit is
  $I\to 2x^2/9$, not the $x^2/2$ given in the paper.
\item The review arXiv:2109.01398 has two sign errors, in eqs.~(4.10) and (4.12); after
  correction, the kernel agrees with an independent quadrature to $2\times10^{-15}$.
  Domènech's large-$x$ asymptotic form is only about a 5\% oracle at fixture $x$ for squeezed
  shapes, and is $O(1)$ off near the resonance $c_s(u+v)=1$. We therefore score against a
  quadrature of his exact kernel, not the asymptotic form.
\item The pipeline's declared error grows like $x\varepsilon$, from about $4\times10^{-8}$ at
  $x=1.6\times10^8$ to a projected $\sim10^{-3}$ at production $x\sim4\times10^{12}$.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Caveat: realistic inputs.}
The oracle tests use exact closed-form $G$ and $T$. They validate the quadrature machinery,
not the stored production functions. With production-like interpolated inputs (so far at $b=0$
only) the error splits additively into two parts:
\begin{itemize}
\item a \emph{representation} term (interpolated $G$, $T$ against exact) of
  $4\times10^{-5}$ to $3\times10^{-3}$, flat in $x$. This is an upper bound, because the fixture
  still uses an older $G$-phase construction;
\item a \emph{$T_k$ seam-clamp} term (the numeric $\to$ WKB hand-over) of $4\times10^{-3}$ to
  $0.9$.
\end{itemize}
On production rows with a clamp gap, the deviation from the radiation oracle is $0.07$--$0.46$,
against $6\times10^{-5}$ for rows without one. \textbf{The seam clamp is the dominant known
error; its fix is planned but has not started.}
\src{docs/handover/REALISTIC-LARGE-X.md, docs/source-remediation-verification.md}

\section{Performance and integrator comparisons}

\paragraph{Levin against adaptive Gauss--Kronrod (\texttt{scipy.quad}).}
On benchmark oscillatory integrals, \texttt{quad} fails between $\omega=10^5$ and $10^6$: it
saturates at about 1\,s and $2\times10^6$ evaluations while returning results with median
relative error $O(10^2)$ or worse. Levin stays accurate at a flat cost of a few ms
(Table~\ref{tab:levin}). On three-Bessel integrals with $10^6$--$2\times10^8$ oscillations,
Levin takes 0.2--1\,s at relative error $10^{-8}$--$6\times10^{-7}$, while \texttt{quad} converged
in 0 of 12 cases within a 60\,s budget. For a purely linear phase, QAWO beats both, as expected.
\src{docs/adaptive-levin-benchmark/LEVIN-PERFORMANCE-REPORT.md}

\paragraph{Levin against a published trapezoid scheme.}
The test case is the radiation-era kernel from a published QCD-crossover kernel paper. Both
methods were scored against a composite Gauss--Legendre reference that is converged to
$10^{-14}$. The paper's trapezoid rule takes 0.4--25\,ms with errors of $10^{-5}$--$6\times10^{-2}$.
Levin, at production settings, reaches $2\times10^{-16}$--$6\times10^{-12}$ at a flat
0.5--0.7\,s. We have not yet run a direct head-to-head of Levin against Gauss--Legendre as a
production integrator.
\src{docs/one-loop-planning/QCD-CROSSOVER-KERNELS-REVIEW.md}

\paragraph{Pipeline costs.}
\begin{itemize}
\item A WKB $G_k$ object fell from 64\,s to about 1\,ms, and a WKB $T_k$ object from 58\,s to
  about 0.05\,s.
\item A typical source-integral row takes 0.1\,s (median) and 0.5\,s (90th percentile).
\item \emph{Production bottleneck}: the $\Lambda$CDM baseline build was stopped at 7{,}600 of
  9{,}280 rows, after 98 CPU-hours. On squeezed triangles ($r\approx k\gg q$) at low $z$, the
  adaptive bisection reaches its depth cap. These rows average 171\,s each, against 0.8\,s for
  the rest, and account for 97.5 of the 98 CPU-hours.
\item Relaxing the quadrature rtol from $10^{-8}$ to $10^{-7}$ gives a $6.8\times$ speedup and
  changes the results by at most $2\times10^{-6}$. This cuts the projected full rebuild from
  about 1{,}000 to about 250 CPU-hours (roughly 36 wall-hours), and is now the default.
\item The underlying limit on squeezed triangles is the phase representation, which delivers
  about six digits; the quadrature method itself is not the limit.
\end{itemize}
\src{prompts/handover/IMPLEMENTATION_STATE.md, docs/handover/QUADSOURCE-TOLERANCE-SWEEP.md}

\section{Main open items}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{$T_k$ seam clamp}: the dominant error, at $0.07$--$0.46$ on affected production
  rows.
\item \textbf{Coverage in $x$}: the oracle tests reach $x\approx1.6\times10^8$, against
  $\sim4\times10^{12}$ in production, where the $x\varepsilon$ term alone is about $10^{-3}$.
\item \textbf{Coverage in $b$ and background}: realistic inputs have not yet been scored at
  $b\neq0$. All the oracles assume constant $w$, so there is no oracle for $\Lambda$CDM or for
  the QCD era; there we rely on self-convergence.
\item \textbf{QCD sound speed}: the transfer sector uses $p/\rho$ in place of
  $\mathrm{d}p/\mathrm{d}\rho$, which differ by up to 23\%. This produces up to $O(10)$\,rad of
  sound-phase error at high $k$, and affects every stored QCD $T_k$.
\end{enumerate}
