## 0. The answers

**The target rule gives `unchanged`, and it is the campaign's third** (README §6.1 rule 4).
`DEFAULT_QUADRATURE_ATOL = 1e-32` and `DEFAULT_QUADRATURE_RTOL = 1e-8` are not the limiting
parameters of the source integral: the representation floor the realistic fixture carries dominates
the quadrature error at the production pair by **×3.46e+04 to ×2.15e+11** over eighteen cases (§8).
The ratio is the largest of the campaign's three `unchanged` findings by four orders — prompt 03's
consumer spline dominated by ×631–×37,700, prompt 04's accumulation floor by ×48.4–×3.03e5.

**So §7 D4 is not reopened.** Prompt 06 §9 makes "the quadrature tolerance *is* the limiting
parameter" a stop that puts D4 back to the user. It is not, by ten orders, and the boundary §0.4
drew on the premise that these two constants were already chosen by measurement is confirmed by a
second, independent measurement.

**Four findings go to the owning campaigns** (§10), and the second is the one that matters most to
a future reader:

1. **`rtol` is the binding parameter and `atol` is inert** — the reverse of the regime
   `source-remediation` log 12 measured. `atol` binds only at `1e-16` and looser, twenty decades
   above the quantity being integrated; below that, twenty-eight decades of it change nothing (§4).
   `rtol` moves the quadrature error by about a decade per decade over the whole axis (§5), and the
   corners show the two never interact: whichever term of `atol + rtol|value|` is larger binds, one
   at a time, and at production it is `rtol` (§6).
2. **`source-remediation` log 12's `1e-8 → 1e-11` bit-identity does not transfer to this tree**, and
   prompt 06 §3 named this as the single most likely error in the prompt. It was measured at
   `atol = 1e-25`, where `atol` bound and `rtol` could not be seen; at `1e-32` the same three
   decades move `total` by a factor of **274**. That is a change of regime, not a contradiction —
   the live measurement was right about the tree it was taken on — but anything that *reasons* from
   that bit-identity today is reasoning from a superseded constant.
3. **`analytic_rad`, the stored oracle column, is what the tolerance pair really moves.** It is
   computed inside `evaluate_QuadSource_integral` at the caller's own pair, and at production it
   sits up to **3.84e-09** of scale from its converged value — up to **×1.25e+06** the quadrature
   error of `total` on the same case (§2.1). Anyone reading a stored `analytic_rad` as a fixed
   oracle is reading a quantity that depends on that row's `atol_serial` and `rtol_serial`.
4. **`DEFAULT_LEVIN_MAX_DEPTH = 20` and `limit = 100` were never chosen**, and are recorded rather
   than touched (`TOLERANCE-INVENTORY.md` §5.4 rows E and F) so that prompt 06a can carry them into
   `docs/TOLERANCE-PROVENANCE.md` in those words.

**The design is what makes the answer possible, and it is the prompt's own.** The fixture's two
flavours separate the two errors that a live sweep could only measure convolved: the **exact**
flavour, where every ingredient is truth, gives the quadrature error alone, and the **realistic**
flavour at the same tolerance gives the representation floor alone. §6.1 rule 1 asks for the floor
first; the pair is the only instrument in the tree that supplies it separately, which is why the
offline route is the better measurement here and not merely the available one.

**Three qualifications ship with the numbers.** (i) The quadrature error at production is below
what double arithmetic can resolve on **3 of the 18 cases**, so those rows carry a *bound* on the
domination and not an estimate (§8). (ii) The loosest `rtol` that clears the floor on every case is
`1e-5`, and it clears it by only **×3.32** — the loose end of the axis is *not* orders clear, and
§8.1 is the README §6.1 rule 3 ladder that says so rather than the comfortable summary. (iii) The
fixture's Bessel splines are built one order looser than production's, because `Case` passes
`bessel_phase`'s **deprecated and ignored** `atol`/`rtol` instead of `phase_atol`/`amplitude_rtol`
(§11); that is the floor under `analytic_rad` here, and it is recorded rather than repaired,
because `test_quadsource_integral.py` is a module this prompt imports and does not edit.

## 11. What this measurement is, and what it is not
