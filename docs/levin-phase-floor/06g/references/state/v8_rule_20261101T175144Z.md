# levin-phase-floor 06g §5, addendum 2 — the V8 sample: rule and fields (written 20261101T175144Z)

> Written before any reading of these calls (their captures run in part 1 with V3's; their readings
> in part 2, after V3's 77 and V1's 12). Read-only from here. Source: the user's ruling, addendum 2
> (`review/dispatch_references_addendum2.md`, SHA-256 c4dcd6dc...). It changes nothing of V3.

**The calls.** The 12 of `review/v8_sample.json` (SHA-256 874e17db..., checked by
`select_refs.py`), not reselected, ids v00..v11 in the file's order (1e-7 q = 0, 0.2, ... 1, then
1e-8). Each is matched in the g census and in 06h's census like V3's calls (rtol, row, call index,
label, span), and both records must equal the file's `g` and `06h` value and abserr. A V8 call that
is also one of V3's 90 is captured and read once, under its V3 id (`out/sample/v8_aliases.json`), and
scored in both tables independently.

**The captures.** At g, from the code worktree with g's census harness (the census's byte for byte,
`out/cmd/12`), exactly as V3's; each checked bit for bit against the g census record (value, abserr,
label, converged, num_regions, max_depth, the region arrays). A capture that does not reproduce its
record is a stop.

**The readings.** Route B (they are cosmological groups; `route_of` decides, and a call on another
route is reported). The four readings of `tools.filon_reference` (QAWO and FCC, resolutions 1 and 2),
each in its own process, stopped at 30 minutes, into `out/readings/vNN-<impl>-r<res>.json` (or the
V3 id's files for an alias).

**The count rule (the brief's, unchanged; never loosened):** all four readings present, their
scatter at most min(abserr_06h, abserr_g)/100, and on Route B every reading's `all_monotone` true.
A call that does not count is recorded with its four readings and their scatter.

**The fields** (`out/v8_sample.json`, table `out/v8_sample.md`, separate from V3's; no prediction):
per call, its id, unit, index, rtol, q and class from the file; its four readings (`value_30`,
`value_hex`, wall time), their scatter and the tolerance, whether it counted; for each counted call
the reference (FCC res 2, hex and 30 digits); 06h's value, abserr and converged (06h's census
record); g's value, abserr and converged (the g census record); |v - ref|/abserr for each code. Every
value in `float.hex`. The question per call: does g's value lie within its declared error of the
reference, and does 06h's? Numbers only; interpretation is the laptop's.
